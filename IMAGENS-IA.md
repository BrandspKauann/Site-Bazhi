# Imagens editoriais da Bazhi

Duas imagens ilustrativas criadas por IA para o site local. Não representam clientes, colaboradores ou depoimentos reais. A identificação de imagem ilustrativa está presente na página.

## Arquivos utilizados

- `dist/assets/bazhi-team-editorial.png`: grupo de profissionais em colaboração, imagem principal.
- `dist/assets/bazhi-professional-editorial.png`: profissional em conversa de negócios, seção humana.

Criadas com a ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens, sem usar uma API externa configurada pelo usuário.

## Prompt da imagem principal

Use case photorealistic-natural. Wide cinematic editorial photograph for Brazilian enterprise B2B software website. Three realistic Brazilian professionals aged 28-45, a woman with naturally curly dark hair and medium brown skin, a light-brown skinned man, and another woman, collaborating around one laptop in a contemporary bright São Paulo office. Authentic candid moment, focused relaxed faces, individual natural skin texture, tasteful everyday business casual charcoal and ivory outfits with subtle crimson accent, real human anatomy and natural hands, thoughtful collaboration rather than posed advertising smiles. Camera at desk eye level, 50mm editorial photography, believable daylight from left, clean offwhite architectural background with soft shadows and subtle glass, sophisticated corporate aesthetic, no futuristic holograms, no robots, no cartoon, no text, no logos, no flags. Horizontal 16:9 composition, subjects across center and right, beautiful photographic detail, room for a dark gradient at bottom when placed on website.

Modern understated office, warm ivory wall and glass, a subtle red folder on desk, bright diffused window light, authentic candid photography not a posed headshot, 50mm lens, sharp eyes, subtle background bokeh, realistic human proportions, high end corporate magazine art direction. Landscape 4:3 composition with woman on right and laptop lower left. No text, no logos, no fake dashboard overlays, no cartoon, no robots. Serious, human, warm and contemporary.
